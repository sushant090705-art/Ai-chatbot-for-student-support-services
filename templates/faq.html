Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about college life, academics, and administration.

Sessional and external exam schedules, along with seating plans, are posted on departmental notice boards and uploaded to the college ERP portal. You can also query the AI Chatbot on your dashboard for live updates.
The Training & Placement (T&P) cell coordinates all campus placement activities. Company eligibility requirements, schedules, and register links are distributed via your student email portal and official Telegram/WhatsApp groups.
Do not panic. Bank gateways typically resolve failed transactions within 24 to 48 working hours. If the status doesn't change to "Paid" on your student ledger after 2 days, take your bank statement receipt directly to the Accounts Office.
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each individual course. Shortfalls below 75% require official medical certificates or authorized duty leaves submitted to your Head of Department (HOD) for review.
Official roll numbers are assigned by the Registrar's Office after document verification completes. Identity cards are issued by the library or your respective department coordinator within the first month of registration.
Undergraduate engineering students can borrow up to 3 books simultaneously for a period of 14 days. Renewals can be requested twice if no reservations exist on those books. Late fees apply for overdue materials.

Application calls for state scholarships (like Digital Gujarat, UP Scholarship, etc.) or merit-cum-means awards are announced annually. Necessary verification forms, income certs, and mark sheets must be submitted to the Student Welfare section.
